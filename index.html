How the group works

Ownership and operations are kept in separate companies. Nexa Line holds the assets, and each operating company runs its own business on equipment it leases.

  1. Nexa Line Ltd
    Owns

    Buys equipment with no loans against it and keeps it maintained to commercial standards.

  2. Lease
    Pays per kilometre

    Group companies pay for the equipment by the kilometre they use it.

  3. Operating company
    Runs the business

    Hires the drivers, serves the customers and builds its own brand.

  4. Reinvest
    Grows the fleet

    Lease income funds the next asset and the next company.
